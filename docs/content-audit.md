## Open questions to settle

1. **Brand capitalisation.** The site renders "LeanStart" but both legal pages say "Leanstart
   Africa". One of the two should change.
2. **www or not.** The Legal Notice gives `https://www.leanstart.africa`; every canonical URL on
   the site points at `https://leanstart.africa` without the www. Pick one and make the other
   redirect to it.
3. **The three social accounts beyond YouTube** came from the redesign hand-off and have not been
   confirmed to exist. They are published as official accounts in the structured data, so a dead
   link is worth more than a cosmetic fix.
4. **Google Fonts** is the one third party the site loads. If the Privacy Policy is meant to be
   complete, it should say so; self-hosting the font would remove the question entirely.
5. **No contact route.** There is no contact page, email link or form anywhere on the site. That is
   a findability gap for anyone who wants to reach you, and the Legal Notice email will become the
   only route once it is filled in.
